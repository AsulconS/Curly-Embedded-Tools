Reports immediates that do not fit the field the instruction encodes them in:
  • add/sub/cmp take a 12-bit immediate, optionally shifted left by 12;
  • movz/movk/movn take a 16-bit immediate shifted by 0, 16, 32 or 48;
  • tbz/tbnz take a bit index within the operand width;
  • shift amounts must be smaller than the operand width.

Only literal constants are checked; expressions involving symbols are left alone.
